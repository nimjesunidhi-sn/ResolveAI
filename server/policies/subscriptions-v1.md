# First charge window
A digital subscription may be considered for a refund within 7 calendar days of its first charge when account usage is below 20 percent. Confirm the first charge date and account usage.

# Later requests
Requests after the 7-day window or with usage at or above 20 percent require a human agent to review. Cancellation and refund are separate actions.
