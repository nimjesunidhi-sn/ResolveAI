# Approval required
Requests over the configured approval limit, suspected fraud, inconsistent customer and order ownership, missing required evidence, and policy exceptions require human approval.

# Decision boundary
An AI generated explanation or retrieved policy passage is not approval. Only deterministic server-side eligibility rules can recommend eligibility, and configured human approval remains required for flagged cases.
