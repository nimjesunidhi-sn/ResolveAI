# Delayed shipments
A full refund may be considered when a verified shipment is more than 14 calendar days past its promised delivery date. The order must still be checked for current carrier status.

# Missing and lost packages
An order that remains in transit or has uncertain tracking must be reviewed by an agent. A lost order may be considered for a refund after the carrier confirms loss. Never infer loss only from a customer's report.
